⛳ POCKET GOLF

Strokes 0 / 6 Total 0 Mulligans 6

Legend & rules

Move 1–6 spaces (d6) in a straight line, 8 directions. Fairway adds +1 and lets you fly over trees; sand takes −1; water and trees can be flown over but never landed on. A putt (1 space, or 1–2 on the green) is always allowed instead of the roll. Slope arrows carry the ball after it lands. Par 6 every hole; mulligans re-roll the die.

Clubs (pick before rolling): Driver adds +1 but needs the tee or fairway; Iron moves exactly your swing; Wedge moves half the roll (rounded up), flies over trees from anywhere, and ignores wind and the sand penalty.

Wind (arrows by the hole number) pushes shots of 4+ spaces one space downwind per arrow after landing — never into water or trees, and never out of the cup.
